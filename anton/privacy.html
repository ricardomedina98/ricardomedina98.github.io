Anton · Privacy policy

Anton is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • Data read from Google APIs (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, People) is used only to assist the owner and stays on the owner's own devices and server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Anton's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
